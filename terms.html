Terms of use

Chill Drive is a personal utility for its owner's server and Google Drive account. It is not offered as a public storage service.

Authorized use

Use is limited to files the owner is entitled to store and manage. Google Drive access requires the account owner's consent and remains subject to Google's applicable terms and storage limits.

Operation and availability

The owner controls configuration, uploads, downloads and deletion. Availability depends on Google Drive, the server and network access. This utility provides no uptime guarantee or guarantee against data loss; important files should have independent backups.

Stopping use

The owner can stop the server service and revoke access in Google Account settings at any time. Existing files remain subject to the owner's retention choices and Google's service rules.

For data handling, see the privacy policy. For general questions, use the project issue tracker.
